# Kema Mate: friend handover

The intended user is a friend who wants meals to support their workout routine,
with a daily food budget of Rs. 2,000 and no special cooking restrictions.
No name, measurements, allergy, or completed handover has been supplied.

1. Register and enter the friend's actual measurements, activity, and goal.
2. Set daily budget to LKR 2,000 and choose the friend's actual diet and cuisines.
3. Add actual allergies and exclusions; none are preselected.
4. Generate a one-day plan first. Show daily estimated calories and cost.
5. Rate one meal and explain a preference, then generate another plan.
6. Swap a meal and confirm updated totals and groceries.
7. Ask what was useful, what was unrealistic, and whether they would use it again.

Record actual results here after the handover. Do not present this checklist as
completed testing or invent quotations or improvements.

For the challenge post, explain why using a replaceable open-weight Gemma model
matters. This implementation uses hosted inference; profile context and feedback
included in the prompt are sent to Google. It is not an offline/private-local app.
